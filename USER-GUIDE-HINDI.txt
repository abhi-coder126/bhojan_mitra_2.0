===============================================================================
PART 1 - SABSE PEHLE: YE SOFTWARE HAI KYA
===============================================================================

RestroSethu ek restaurant billing + management system hai. Isme do alag hisse hain:

  1. STAFF APP (staff wala hissa)
     Login karke khulta hai. Isme billing, orders, kitchen, menu, stock,
     reports - sab kuch hai.

  2. CUSTOMER QR MENU (customer wala hissa)
     Isme koi login nahi. Customer table par rakha QR scan karta hai, menu
     dekhta hai, aur apne phone se hi order kar deta hai.

Ek hi software me aap kai branch (outlets) chala sakte ho. Har branch ka data
doosri branch se poori tarah alag rehta hai - ek branch ka staff doosri branch
ka kuch bhi nahi dekh sakta.


===============================================================================
PART 2 - SOFTWARE CHALU KAISE KAREIN
===============================================================================

Iske do hisse alag-alag chalte hain, aur dono ka chalna zaroori hai:

  Pehli window:   cd backend    phir   npm run dev
  Doosri window:  cd frontend   phir   npm run dev

Phir browser me kholo:  http://localhost:5173

*** BAHUT ZAROORI BAAT ***
Agar backend (pehli window) band hai, to login page to khul jayega lekin login
nahi hoga. "Login kyu nahi ho raha" ki sabse aam wajah yahi hai. Pehle check
karo ki backend chal raha hai ya nahi.

*** DOOSRI ZAROORI BAAT ***
Agar aapne code me change kiya hai aur browser me purana hi dikh raha hai, to
browser me hard refresh karo: Ctrl + Shift + R

PART 5 - RESTAURANT ORDERS (roz ka sabse zyada use hone wala page)
===============================================================================

Yahi asli kaam ki screen hai. Upar CHAAR view hain, har ek par icon hai:

  Table View            - table ka naksha. Jis table par order hai wo highlight
                          hogi. Jab QR se naya order aata hai to table chamakti
                          hai aur counter par alarm bajta hai.
  Running Orders        - abhi chal rahe saare orders ki list
  Takeaway & Delivery   - NAYA. Takeaway aur delivery ke orders yahan dikhte
                          hain. Ye orders kisi table par nahi baithte, isliye
                          table view me nahi dikhte the. Tab par ek number bhi
                          dikhta hai - kitne aise order abhi chal rahe hain.
                          Andar do button hain (dono par icon):
                            Takeaway (bag icon)  - counter se le jaane wale
                            Delivery (bike icon) - ghar bhejne wale
                          Kisi ek par click karke uske orders dekho. Har card par
                          status, customer, items, kisne order liya, total, aur
                          agli stage ka button + Bill milta hai.
  Invoices              - ban chuke bill

--- NAYA ORDER BANANA ---
  "New Order" button dabao. Usme:
      Order Type       - Dine-in (table par) / Takeaway (le jaane wala) /
                         Delivery (ghar bhejna). Teeno par icon hai.
      Table            - kaunsi table (sirf dine-in ke liye)
      Delivery Address - pata (delivery ke liye zaroori hai)
      Customer         - customer ka naam
  Phir items jodo aur order save kar do.

--- ORDER KI STAGE (status) ---
  Order in stages se guzarta hai:

      new  ->  accepted  ->  preparing  ->  ready  ->  served

      new        - abhi-abhi aaya, counter ne dekha nahi
      accepted   - counter ne accept kar liya
      preparing  - kitchen me ban raha hai
      ready      - ban gaya, serve karna baaki hai
      served     - de diya gaya
      cancelled  - cancel

  Dhyan do: ek hi status alag-alag naam se dikhta hai - dine-in me "served",
  delivery me "Delivered", takeaway me "picked up". Andar se teeno ek hi
  cheez hain, bas shabd alag hai.

--- AUR KYA-KYA OPTION HAIN ---
  Bill            - bill banana / print karna
  Apply Discount  - chhoot dena
  Merge Bill      - do order ka bill ek saath jod dena
                    (jaise do table wale ek saath payment karna chahein)
  Split Bill      - ek bill ko alag-alag baant dena
  Hold            - order ko rok dena
  Resume          - roka hua order dobara chalu karna
  Delete          - order hatana (password poocha jayega)

  Product name             - item ka naam

                             (Category ki photo yahan nahi - wo "Add Category"

PART 9 - SMART INVENTORY (kachche maal ka stock)
===============================================================================

Ye sabse kaam ki cheezon me se ek hai. Yahan kachcha maal (paneer, tomato, tel,
chawal) track hota hai - aur sabse badi baat, ye khud-ba-khud ghatta hai.

YE KAISE APNE AAP HOTA HAI:
  Aap har dish ki "recipe" bana dete ho - jaise "ek paneer tikka me 200 gram
  paneer aur 50 ml tel lagta hai". Iske baad jab bhi wo dish bikegi, utna
  kachcha maal apne aap stock se kam ho jayega. Har bikri ke baad haath se
  stock ghatane ki zaroorat nahi.

Upar paanch tab hain:

  Stock        - saara kachcha maal, kitna bacha hai, uska daam aur kul keemat.
                 Har line par button: Purchase (kharid darj karo),
                 Wastage (barbadi darj karo), Count (ginti milao),
                 History (itihaas dekho), edit (badlo), delete (hatao)
  Recipes      - kaunsi dish me kaunsa maal kitna lagta hai
  Food Cost    - ek dish banane me asal me kitna kharch aata hai
  Stock Ledger - har ghatne-badhne ka pura hisab: kab, kyun, kitna
  Reorder      - jo maal khatam hone wala hai

REORDER TAB (khatam hone wala maal):
  Upar laal patti me likha aata hai ki kitne item mangwane hain aur kitne
  bilkul khatam ho chuke hain. Har line par:
      "Low" ya "Out of Stock" ka nishan
      "Short by 3.5 kg" - yani kitna kam pad raha hai, taaki aapko hisab na
      lagana pade ki kitna mangwana hai
      "Record purchase" button - ek click me kharid darj karo

  Har maal ke liye "Reorder level" set hota hai. Stock usse neeche girte hi wo
  maal is tab me aa jata hai.

Upar ke card me dikhta hai: Stock value (kul stock ki keemat), Need reorder,
Purchased (30 days), Used in orders (30 days), Wastage (30 days)

PART 11C - ITEM OFFERS (Menu Items > "Add Offer")
===============================================================================

Coupon poore bill par chhoot deta hai. ITEM OFFER ek khaas item par hota hai -
jaise "Buy 1 Get 1 Free" ya "pizza ke saath cold drink free".

KAHAN SE BANTA HAI:
  Menu Items kholo -> upar "Add Offer" button (Add Category ke bagal me).

DO TARAH KE OFFER HAIN:

  1. BUY 1 GET 1 FREE
     Ek hi item ka doosra free. Jaise "Farmhouse Pizza - Buy 1 Get 1".

  2. FREE ITEM WITH THIS
     Ek item khareedne par DOOSRA item free. Jaise "pizza ke saath
     Cold Drink (250 ml) free".

BHARNA KYA HAI:
  Offer title     - jo customer ko dikhega, jaise "Buy 1 Get 1 Free"
  Type            - upar wale do me se ek
  Buy this item   - kis item par offer hai
  Which sizes?    - AGAR us item me sizes hain to yahan tick karo ki offer
                    kin sizes par chalega. Jaise BOGO sirf Medium aur Large
                    par - Regular par nahi. Kuch bhi tick na karo to SAARE
                    sizes par chalega.
  Free item       - (sirf combo me) kya free milega, aur uska size
  Runs on         - hafte ke kin dino chalega (Indian time)
  Active          - chalu hai ya paused

CUSTOMER KO KAHAN DIKHTA HAI:
  Menu me "Offers for you" strip me, sabse aage. Card par "BUY 1 GET 1" ya
  "FREE ITEM" ka tag, offer ka naam, aur kin sizes par hai wo likha aata hai.
  Card par click karo to seedha us item ka popup khul jata hai.

ORDER ME APNE AAP LAGTA HAI:
  Customer jab qualifying item order karta hai, free item apne aap order me
  jud jata hai - daam ZERO par. Matlab:
      - Kitchen ko ticket par dikh jata hai ki DO pizza banane hain
      - Customer se sirf ek ka paisa liya jata hai
  Ye counter par yaad rakhne ki cheez nahi hai, system khud karta hai.

PART 12 - COUPONS (offer aur coupon)
===============================================================================

Customer ko chhoot dene ke liye coupon yahan bante hain.

Ek coupon banate samay ye option hain:

  Code                 - coupon code, jaise WELCOME20
                         (2 se 40 akshar, sirf English letters, digits, - aur _)
  Title                - offer ka naam, jo customer ko dikhega
  Description          - chhota sa vivran
  Discount type        - Amount (seedhe rupaye) ya Percent (percent)
  Discount value       - kitni chhoot (percent me 1 se 100 tak)
  Minimum bill amount  - itne se upar ka bill hone par hi chalega
  Usage limit          - kul kitni baar use ho sakta hai (0 = unlimited)
  Start / End date     - kab se kab tak chalega
  Available days       - hafte ke kin dino chalega (Indian time ke hisab se)
  "Show in Top Offers on the customer menu"
                       - ise tick karne par ye offer customer ke menu me sabse
                         upar "Top Offers" me dikhega.
                         Tick na karo to ye chhupa rahega - sirf usi ko milega
                         jise code pata hai.
  Status               - Active ya Inactive

COUPON KAISE LAGTA HAI (customer ki taraf):
  Customer menu me upar "Top Offers" dekhta hai, ya khud code type karta hai,
  phir "Apply" dabata hai.

  Zaroori baat: code daal kar Apply dabana zaroori hai. Agar code box me likha
  hai par Apply nahi dabaya, to order place nahi hoga - button band rahega. Ye
  jaanbujh kar rakha gaya hai taaki customer ko chhoot mile bina order na chala
  jaye.

  Agar Apply karne ke baad customer cart me kuch badalta hai, to coupon hat
  jayega aur dobara lagana padega - taaki chhoot hamesha sahi rakam par lage.

  Coupon ki jaanch do baar hoti hai: ek baar Apply dabane par, aur doosri baar
  order place hote samay. Isliye limit khatam ho chuka coupon aakhri waqt par
  bhi nahi chalega.

PART 19 - NAYA ORDER AANE PAR ALARM (tune) KAISE KAAM KARTA HAI
===============================================================================

Jab customer QR se order karta hai, counter ko turant pata chalna chahiye.
Iske liye teen cheezein ek saath hoti hain:

  1. TUNE BAJTI HAI
     public/ordertune.mp3 loop me bajti rehti hai jab tak order dekh na lo.
     Poore system me ek hi audio chalti hai, isliye do jagah se ek saath
     bajkar aawaz nahi tootegi.

  2. POPUP AATA HAI
     Screen par naye order ka popup, aur Table View me wo table chamakti hai.

  3. LIST APNE AAP REFRESH HOTI HAI
     Har kuch second me nayi list aa jati hai - F5 dabane ki zaroorat nahi.

SETTINGS SE CONTROL:  Settings -> Notifications & Display
     Sound on/off        - tune bajegi ya nahi
     Repeat sound        - ek baar bajegi ya bar-bar (jab tak dekh na lo)
     Popup on/off        - popup dikhega ya nahi
     Refresh seconds     - kitne second me list refresh ho (kam se kam 3)
     Test Sound ka button bhi hai - ek baar bajakar dekh lo.

*** ZAROORI: TUNE NAHI BAJ RAHI? ***
  Browser ka niyam hai ki page par ek baar click/tap kiye bina aawaz nahi
  baja sakte. Agar page khol kar chhod diya aur kuch chhua nahi, to pehli
  tune block ho sakti hai. System aapse ek tap maangega - ek baar tap kar
  do, uske baad sab apne aap bajega.

"Login nahi ho raha"
   -> backend chal raha hai ya nahi, ye check karo. Ye sabse aam wajah hai.

"Browser me naya change dikh hi nahi raha"
   -> Ctrl + Shift + R se hard refresh karo. Tab bahut der se khula ho to
      purana version dikhta rehta hai.
   -> Agar aap deployed (online) site dekh rahe ho, to naya code tabhi dikhega
      jab changes push/deploy ho chuke hon.

"Customer ka order nahi aa raha"
   -> Dekho ki QR sahi branch aur table ka hai.
   -> Restaurant Orders screen khuli hai ya nahi (alarm wahin bajta hai).

"Coupon nahi lag raha"
   -> Coupon ka Status Active hai?
   -> Aaj ka din "Available days" me chuna hua hai?
   -> Bill "Minimum bill amount" se upar hai?
   -> Usage limit khatam to nahi ho gaya?
   -> Start/End date ke beech me hai?

"Customer ko size (Regular/Medium/Large) ya add-on ka option nahi dikh raha"
   -> Ye har item ke liye ALAG SE bharna padta hai, apne aap nahi aata.
      Menu Items -> us item par Edit -> MRP ke theek neeche "Sizes & prices"
      section hai. Category "Pizza" likhoge to seedha + Regular / + Medium /
      + Large ke button mil jayenge - bas daba kar daam bhar do.
      Usi ke neeche "Add-ons & extras" hai (extra cheese, dip waqaira).
   -> Jis item me sizes nahi bhare, uska popup sirf seedha daam dikhayega.
      Ye galti nahi hai - har item me size hona zaroori nahi (jaise ek
      coffee me shayad ho, ek dessert me nahi).

"Stock apne aap kam nahi ho raha"
   -> Us dish ki Recipe bani hai ya nahi, ye check karo. Recipe ke bina system
      ko pata hi nahi chalega ki kaunsa maal kitna ghatana hai.

"Bill delete karna hai"
   -> Invoices screen se bill delete nahi ho sakta, ye jaanbujh kar band hai.
      Galat order ko Restaurant Orders se cancel kiya jaa sakta hai.
